Bluetooth permission

Unspied needs Bluetooth access to observe nearby Bluetooth Low Energy broadcasts and perform supported privacy analysis.

Local Network permission

Local Network access is used only when you run Deep Privacy Check. It allows Unspied to discover selected Bonjour services advertised on your local network.

Subscription and 3-day trial

For new users, an active Apple subscription is required after onboarding. Monthly and Yearly plans unlock the same full Unspied feature set. Eligible Apple accounts can receive a 3-day free trial on the selected plan before automatic renewal begins.

Apple allows one introductory offer per subscription group. If an account has already used the introductory offer, Apple may charge the selected subscription immediately when the purchase is confirmed.

Cancel, restore, or manage a subscription

Use Restore purchases in Unspied if a current Apple entitlement is not showing. Use Manage subscription to open Apple's subscription management screen. Cancelling stops future renewal according to Apple's subscription rules.

What does “Unknown” mean?

Unspied does not claim that a detected device is recording. Recording state remains unknown unless evidence can genuinely establish otherwise.

No clues found?

A clear scan does not guarantee that a space is free of cameras, microphones, or other recording devices. Some devices do not expose discoverable Bluetooth or network services.

How Unspied interprets results

Bluetooth names, manufacturer identifiers, signal strength, advertised services, discovered GATT services, and local-network service advertisements can provide useful clues.

A vendor identifier does not by itself prove a specific device model, purpose, camera capability, or recording state. Unspied intentionally uses conservative wording to avoid presenting a clue as proof.

Important: Unspied is a best-effort wireless privacy scanner. It is not a guarantee that every hidden camera, microphone, smart glasses device, or other privacy-relevant device will be detected.
