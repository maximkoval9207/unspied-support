Overview

Unspied is designed to perform supported wireless scanning and analysis locally on your device. The app does not require an account or a Unspied cloud service to perform its scanning functions.

1. Information processed on your device

To provide its scanning features, Unspied may process wireless observations that are visible to your device, including:

  • Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements;
  • advertised device names, when available;
  • Bluetooth manufacturer identifiers;
  • advertised Bluetooth service UUIDs;
  • approximate Bluetooth signal strength (RSSI);
  • supported GATT service information discovered during Deep Privacy Check;
  • selected Bonjour service advertisements visible on your local network.

These observations are used to generate scan results and privacy-related clues inside the app.

2. Data collection by Unspied

Unspied does not transmit your scan observations to a Unspied server. We do not operate an account system or scanning backend for the app.

We do not sell scan data, use scan data for advertising, or use scan data to track you across apps or websites.

3. Local storage

Certain app preferences may be stored locally on your device, such as your selected language and information you choose to mark as familiar. Local app data remains under the control of your device and may be removed when the app or its local data is deleted.

4. Bluetooth permission

Bluetooth permission is used to observe nearby Bluetooth Low Energy broadcasts and provide Bluetooth-based scanning features.

5. Local Network permission

Local Network access is requested when Deep Privacy Check is used. The permission allows Unspied to discover selected services advertised through Bonjour on the local network visible to your device.

6. No precise-location claim

Bluetooth signal strength is approximate and can be affected by walls, people, interference, device orientation, radio conditions, and other factors. Unspied does not present Bluetooth RSSI as an exact physical distance.

7. Device identification and recording state

A Bluetooth company identifier, device name, wireless service, or other protocol clue does not necessarily identify an exact physical device or model.

Unspied does not treat a wireless clue as proof that recording is occurring. Recording state may remain unknown.

8. Detection limitations

Unspied does not guarantee detection of all cameras, microphones, smart glasses, recording devices, or other privacy-relevant devices. Some devices may not advertise Bluetooth or discoverable local-network services, may be powered off, may use unsupported protocols, or may otherwise be invisible to the available scanning methods.

9. Third-party platform services

Distribution of Unspied through Apple's App Store and TestFlight is subject to Apple's own services and privacy practices. Any information processed independently by Apple is governed by Apple's applicable policies and is not controlled by Unspied.

10. Children's privacy

Unspied does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Unspied account or backend service because the app does not operate such services.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Unspied's functionality or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
